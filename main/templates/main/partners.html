BECOME A PARTNER

Build. Learn. Earn. Grow.

The Wapangaji Kiganjani Partner Program is designed to give young people an opportunity to participate in the digital property management ecosystem.

As a Partner, you introduce landlords and property owners to Wapangaji Kiganjani and help them discover an easier way to manage their properties and tenants.

HOW IT WORKS

How You Earn

Becoming a Wapangaji Kiganjani Partner is simple. Follow these steps and start building your network.

01

Register as a Partner

Register through the Partner registration page and create your own Partner account.

02

Get Your Referral Code

After registering, you will find your unique referral code in your Partner back office.

03

Refer Landlords

When a landlord registers on Wapangaji Kiganjani, ask them to enter your referral code during registration.

04

Earn 10%

Whenever a landlord you referred makes a subscription payment, you earn 10% of the subscription amount.

YOUR OPPORTUNITY

The More Landlords You Have, The More You Can Earn

Your earning potential grows as you build your network of landlords.

Every time a landlord you referred makes a subscription payment, you earn a 10% commission from that subscription.

Continue introducing more landlords to Wapangaji Kiganjani and grow your network over time.

YOUR MONEY

Your Earnings. Your Wallet.

Your Partner account is personal to you. When you log into your back office, you can see your referrals and track your earnings.

Your commissions are displayed in your Partner wallet, so you can see how much money you have earned.

When you have available funds, you can withdraw your money and use it for your personal needs.
